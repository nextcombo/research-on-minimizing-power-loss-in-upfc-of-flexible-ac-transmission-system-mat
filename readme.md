# Research on Minimizing Power Loss in UPFC of Flexible AC Transmission System (Matlab Code)

Flexible AC Transmission System (FACTS) - Unified Power Flow Controller (UPFC) is one of the critical devices in power systems. It possesses the capability to control the voltage, power, and current of the power system in real-time, thereby enhancing the stability and reliability of the power system. The UPFC can achieve power flow control by adjusting the phase and magnitude of voltage and current, minimizing power loss. Through this approach, the UPFC can effectively optimize the operation of the power system, improve energy transmission efficiency, and reduce energy loss. Additionally, the UPFC has a rapid response characteristic that allows it to quickly regulate when there are faults or emergencies in the power system, ensuring the stable operation of the grid. Therefore, the UPFC plays an essential role in modern power systems, significantly improving the operational efficiency and reliability of the power system.
The research on minimizing power loss in UPFC mainly includes the following aspects:
1. Control strategy research: By studying the control strategies of UPFC, including methods for adjusting the phase and magnitude of voltage and current, as well as selecting control parameters, the goal is to minimize power loss.
2. Power flow optimization research: By analyzing the power flow of the power system, determining the control objectives of UPFC to achieve power loss minimization.
3. System simulation research: By establishing a power system simulation model, including the UPFC model and the power system model, conducting system simulation studies to analyze the impact of UPFC on power system power loss and optimize the control strategy of UPFC.
4. Experimental verification research: By conducting actual power system experiments to verify the effectiveness of the control strategy of UPFC in minimizing power loss, providing references for practical applications.
Through these researches, it is possible to achieve the control of power loss minimization in UPFC within flexible AC transmission systems, thereby enhancing the stability and reliability of the power system.
The UPFC model is one of the important members of the FACTS (Flexible AC Transmission System) equipment. It is a solid-state converter based on microcontrollers, capable of controlling active and reactive power in transmission lines, controlling power flow and actual power loss. The UPFC model can observe voltage regulation, minimization of power loss and enhancement of power transmission capacity.
